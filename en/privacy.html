Privacy

StageVO is made by VO Makers. Last updated: November 2026.

StageVO does not collect personal data, requires no account and contains no ads or tracking.

Your photos stay on your iPhone. Selecting, placing, erasing and wall painting all run on your device; photos are never sent to a server. You pick photos with the system photo picker, so StageVO only sees the photos you choose. Saving a result asks for permission to add it to your library.

Network. The first time you use Erase, the removal model is downloaded once from GitHub (github.com; if that fails, from huggingface.co as a backup). Only the file is requested; no photos or personal data are sent. As with any internet connection, your IP address is visible to that server during the download.

Purchases are handled by Apple; we have no access to your payment details. The daily free-save counter is kept only on your phone.

Deleting the app deletes all data. Questions: support@vomakers.com
