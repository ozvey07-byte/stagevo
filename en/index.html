FAQ

Only part of the product gets selected. Tap the missing part too, or draw a Box around the whole product.

Something next to it gets selected as well. Choose "Remove" and tap that item.

The product looks pasted on. Open Color on the result screen and adjust "Match room", brightness or shadow.

Something should stay in front of the product. Tap "In front" and tap that item.

A shadow is left after erasing. In Erase mode, paint over the leftover area with your finger.

What's in the free version? Every feature is open. You can save or share 3 results a day, with a small StageVO mark in the corner. Premium removes the limit and the mark.

My Premium purchase is missing. Settings (⚙︎) → Premium → Restore purchases.

How do I cancel? iPhone Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
